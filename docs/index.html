只调三个进气口的气速，让鼓泡塔里的气体分布更均匀。大语言模型写控制程序，真实 CFD 逐轮打分，统计检验决定是否采纳。没有梯度，没有人工调参。Tune only the gas velocity of three inlets to spread the gas evenly through a bubble column. A language model writes the control program, real CFD scores every round, and a statistical test decides what to keep. No gradients. No hand tuning.
